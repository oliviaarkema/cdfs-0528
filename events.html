Events & Experiences

From summer socials to farm tours to Michigan's biggest Christian music festival — there's always something happening at Country Dairy.

All Year Long

More Than a Farm Store —
A Place to Gather

Country Dairy has always been a place where the community comes together. Whether you're bringing your family out for a tour, celebrating summer at the Ice Cream Social, or catching a show at Unity Festival, we're glad you're here.

Annual Summer Event

Ice Cream Social

Every summer, Country Dairy hosts its beloved Ice Cream Social — a celebration of community, farm-fresh food, and of course, ice cream. Guests enjoy live music, lawn games, great food, and all the scoops they can handle, right here on the farm. It's one of the highlights of the West Michigan summer calendar, and we'd love to see you there.

  • Live music and entertainment throughout the day
  • All-you-can-eat ice cream from our own creamery
  • Farm activities and family fun
  • Food vendors and community vendors on site
  • Held annually each summer — watch for dates
A beloved Country Dairy tradition since the very beginning.

Year-Round Experiences

Farm Tours, Moo U & School Trips

Whether you're a family looking for a fun afternoon, a school group planning a field trip, or a church group wanting something memorable — we have an experience designed for you. Our farm education program and guided tours run seasonally and can be booked for groups of all sizes.

Farm Tours
Horse-drawn wagon rides, barn tours, and calf encounters for families and visitors.
Moo University
Hands-on farm ed classes with butter making, milking demos, and live animal encounters.
School Trips
Curriculum-connected field trips for K–12 groups. Custom programs available.

Main Stage Sponsor

Unity Christian
Music Festival

Country Dairy is proud to be the presenting sponsor of the Main Stage at Unity Christian Music Festival — one of the nation's largest Christian music events, held every summer in downtown Muskegon at Heritage Landing Park on the beautiful Muskegon Lake waterfront. We serve our farm-fresh ice cream on-site all weekend long, so you can enjoy a scoop while your favorite artists take the stage.

Main Stage Sponsor 50,000+ Attendees 4 Days of Music 20+ Top Artists Muskegon, MI
  • Country Dairy serves ice cream on-site throughout the festival
  • The Main Stage is presented by Country Dairy
  • Held annually each summer at Heritage Landing, downtown Muskegon
  • Top Christian artists perform across four days
