Accessibility Statement

Last updated: June 2026

Effective Date: June 1, 2026  ·  Country Dairy, Inc.  ·  New Era, Michigan

Country Dairy, Inc. is committed to ensuring that our website is accessible to all users, including individuals with disabilities. We strive to provide a positive experience for everyone and are continually working to improve the accessibility of our digital content.

Our Goal

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C). These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible to people with disabilities. Conformance to these guidelines helps make the web more usable for all people.

Measures We Are Taking

Country Dairy takes the following steps to ensure accessibility of our website:

  • Including meaningful alternative text for images
  • Using sufficient color contrast between text and background
  • Structuring content with semantic HTML to support screen readers
  • Ensuring interactive elements are keyboard-navigable
  • Using ARIA labels where appropriate to improve assistive technology compatibility
  • Designing layouts that are responsive and usable on mobile devices

Known Limitations

While we strive to meet accessibility standards, some content on our Site may not yet fully conform to WCAG 2.1 Level AA. We are actively working to identify and address these gaps. Third-party content, including embedded booking tools and social media feeds, may not meet the same standards, as those are outside our direct control.

Feedback and Contact

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of our website. If you experience any barriers to access or would like to request content in an accessible format, please contact us:

Physical Accessibility

For questions about physical accessibility at our farm, store, or event spaces, please call us at (231) 259-0515. We are happy to make accommodations and answer any questions before your visit.

Formal Complaints

If you are not satisfied with our response to your accessibility concern, you may contact the relevant authorities in your jurisdiction. In the United States, the U.S. Department of Justice handles ADA-related complaints at ada.gov.

Ongoing Commitment

This statement was prepared in June 2026. We review and update our accessibility practices on an ongoing basis and will revise this statement as our work progresses.
