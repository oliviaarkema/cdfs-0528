Farm Fresh Milk

Straight from the Herd

Fresh Michigan Milk

Every drop starts with our own Holstein herd in New Era, non-GMO fed, hormone-free, and processed right on the farm. That means less time in transit and a fresher product on your shelf, every time.

Certified Non-GMO
Our cows are fed a non-GMO verified diet, with no genetically modified feed, ever.
No Artificial Growth Hormones
Just cows being cows, with no rBST or synthetic hormone treatments in our herd.
Farm-Processed & Bottled On-Site
Milked and bottled in New Era so you get the freshest product on the shelf.
SQF Certified & Kosher
Independently audited for food safety and quality. Certified Kosher Dairy.
