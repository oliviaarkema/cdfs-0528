Everything Fresh.
All in One Place.

Step inside the Country Dairy Farm Store for a taste of everything we make right here on the farm. From fresh milk and homemade ice cream to farm-raised meats, artisan cheeses, and our own baked goods, this is your one-stop shop for the best West Michigan has to offer.

We also carry locally sourced goods from neighbors we trust, including honey, maple syrup, and jam. Come browse, grab lunch, or load up the cooler.

Homemade Meals Worth Gathering Around

Our kitchen serves up honest, satisfying food made with the same care as everything else we do. Real beef burgers, fresh salads, hearty wraps and hoagies, and hand-scooped ice cream milkshakes, all made with ingredients sourced close to home.
