Wagon Ride Tour

Behind the Barn

Climb aboard our horse-drawn wagon for a guided tour through the heart of Country Dairy. You'll travel through the farm with our team as your guide, getting a ground-level look at how a modern Michigan dairy actually runs.

  • Horse-drawn wagon ride through the farm with a guided narration
  • Tour through our free-stall dairy barn and see where cows live day-to-day
  • Watch our robotic milking system in action
  • Learn about silage production and how we feed the herd
  • Meet calves and young stock up close
Perfect for families, school groups, and first-time visitors. All ages welcome.

Walking Tour

Hoofin' It

Lace up your boots and step inside one of our milking barns for an up-close look at modern dairy farming. This walking tour puts you right in the middle of the action, where you can see the technology and the tradition that keep this farm running every single day.

  • Walk through an active milking barn with a knowledgeable guide
  • See our state-of-the-art robotic milking system up close
  • Meet our hardworking Holstein cows and learn about their care
  • Spend time with some of the farm's playful calves
  • Great for curious adults and kids who want a hands-on experience
Wear closed-toe shoes. This tour involves walking on active farm surfaces.

Horses & Calves

Birthing Barn & Calves

This is one of the most memorable tours we offer. Travel by horse-drawn wagon to our Maternity Barn, where new life begins on the farm every single day. You'll get up close with newborn calves, learn how we care for them from their very first hours, and see the warmth and work that goes into raising the next generation of our herd.

  • Wagon ride to the Maternity Barn with our team
  • Meet newborn calves and learn about their first days of life
  • Learn how calves are fed, named, and cared for
  • Understand the full lifecycle of a dairy cow
  • An experience kids and adults remember for years
Availability varies based on farm activity. Calves are always present, though newborns depend on timing.

School Groups & Families

Moo U Classes

Moo U is our hands-on farm education program designed for school groups, youth organizations, and curious families. Our team brings the farm to life through interactive lessons, live demonstrations, and up-close animal encounters that connect people to where their food really comes from.

Winter Sleigh Ride

Winter Horse-Drawn Sleigh Rides

Come over the river and through the woods to Country Dairy for a Horse-Drawn Sleigh Ride this winter! Belgian horses pull our sleigh over the snow covered hills and pastures of Oceana County while your family enjoys the peace and beauty of this nostalgic ride. After your 45-minute ride, cozy up in our Moo School for some of our homemade hot chocolate and cookies to complete this fun family experience.

  • Wednesdays through Saturdays, December through February — weather permitting
  • $150 for up to 10 people; additional guests up to 15 at $15/person
  • Book online or call #231-861-4636 ext 119
  • All rides are weather-permitting
Large groups welcome! Call for group pricing and scheduling.
