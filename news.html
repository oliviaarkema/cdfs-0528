Ride the Hart–Montague Trail & Stop at the Farm

The Hart–Montague Trail State Park is one of Michigan's most beloved rail-trails — a 22-mile paved path winding through orchards, farmland, and small towns across Oceana County. Country Dairy sits right along the route, making us a favorite stop for cyclists fueling up with a scoop (or three) of homemade ice cream before the ride back. Whether you're a seasoned cyclist or just looking for a scenic family outing, this trail is a perfect West Michigan summer day.

Dairy April 2025

What Is A2/A2 Milk — and Why Does It Matter?

You may have seen A2 milk showing up on store shelves and wondered what the difference is. The short answer: it comes down to protein. Most conventional milk contains both A1 and A2 beta-casein proteins, but A2/A2 milk comes from cows that only produce the A2 type. Many people who experience discomfort with regular milk find A2 much easier to digest — and the taste is noticeably creamier. We're excited to be exploring what this means for Country Dairy and our herd.

Our Commitment to Non-GMO Milk

At Country Dairy, we believe you deserve to know exactly what's in your milk — and what isn't. Our cows are fed a non-GMO diet, free from genetically modified feed, so every glass you pour starts with feed we feel good about. It's part of a broader commitment to transparency, quality, and doing right by the land and the families we serve across West Michigan.
