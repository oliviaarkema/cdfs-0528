Explore a Real Working Dairy Farm

Come see a real, working Michigan dairy farm in action. Watch the milking process, meet the horses by name, pet the calves, and learn where your food truly comes from. Our farm tours are designed for everyone: curious kids and adults alike.

Book a Tour
Wagon Ride Tour
Behind the Barn

Climb aboard our horse-drawn wagon for a guided tour through the farm, where you’ll learn about silage production, ride through a free-stall dairy barn, meet calves and young stock, and see our robotic milking system in action.

Learn More →
Walking Tour
Hoofin’ It

Step inside one of our milking barns for an up-close look at modern dairy farming. You’ll see our state-of-the-art robotic milking system in action, meet our hardworking Holstein cows, and spend time with some of the farm’s playful calves.

Learn More →
Horses & Calves
Birthing Barn and Calves Tour

Journey to our Maternity Barn by horse-drawn wagon for an up-close look at where new life begins on the farm. Meet newborn calves, learn about their care, and experience one of the most memorable parts of dairy farming.

Our Story

Since 1907, the Van Gunst family has farmed this same land in New Era, Michigan. Four generations of hard work, a deep love for their animals, and a commitment to sharing that life with you. Today, Country Dairy is home to over 1,200 dairy cows and a farm store where the whole family is welcome.
