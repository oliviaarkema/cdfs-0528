Our Story

Since 1907, the Van Gunst family has farmed this same land in New Era, Michigan. Five generations of hard work, a deep love for their animals, and a commitment to sharing that life with you. Today, Country Dairy is home to over 1,200 dairy cows and a farm store where the whole family is welcome.
